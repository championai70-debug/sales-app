Sales App

Load your sales. Pick a budget and time frame. See what to buy.

The app finds your top sellers, compares new articles with similar ones, checks sell-through, and splits your budget into euros per category and units per article.

    How the app decides
    
    
      
  1. Top sellers. A gradient-boosted tree model (the same idea as XGBoost) learns from your history how many units each article sells next, using recent sales, trend, price, margin, age and how its category is doing.
    2. 
      
  2. New articles. Articles without sales get the forecast of their three most similar articles (same category, close price, same brand or colour), reduced by 20% to be careful.
    3. 
      
  3. Sell-through. Units sold in the last 8 weeks divided by units sold plus stock now. Categories that sell through faster get a bigger share of the budget.
    4. 
      
  4. Time frame. The model is trained for the time frame you pick (2 weeks, a month, a season), so it learns how sales move over that span.
    5. 
      
  5. Euros per category. The budget is split by what each category needs to reach your target sell-through, weighted by its sell-through.
    6. 
      
  6. Units per article. Each category's euros go to its articles by need, a little more for top sellers and a little less for slow ones, then are turned into units at cost price.
